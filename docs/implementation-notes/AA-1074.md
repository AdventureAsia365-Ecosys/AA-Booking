# AA-1074 — Booking finance: FX-normalised costs, departure shared costs, purchase orders, planned vs actual, profit

Lane l16 of session S277 (Claude Code). Source of the scope: the S275 study (root
`docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md`, items bk-05, bk-06, bk-07, bk-08, bk-10). Our own
code; nothing of the OPL-1 app is copied.

## Decisions

- **One migration, bk-045, contract.** It ALTERs `supplier_bookings` / `supplier_bills`, replaces three guards and adds
  views next to two new tables, so it is MIG-4: a migration-only PR, then the code PR raises readiness. bk-045 was
  named in CONTEXT for the B2C table drop (planned slot ≥ 01/12); the lane rule "reserved numbers go to owner main
  max + 1" gives 045 to the first file that ships, so the drop takes the next free number. CONTEXT says so.
- **USD rounding rule**: every native amount is converted on its own at its rate and rounded half up to the cent
  (`ROUND_HALF_UP` in Python, `round(numeric, 2)` in SQL); totals are sums of those cents. A departure cost is split
  `net x seats / sold`, half up per case; the rounding remainder stays with the departure (its profit carries the full
  shared cost).
- **Plain rate, no buffer** for costs: the FX buffer is a selling margin, not a cost. `fx_plain_rate` is the SQL twin
  of `app.pricing.fx.plain_rates` (same "newest approved row dated on or before the day").
- **Snapshots**: a supplier booking at confirm (the moment the price is agreed), cleared by a re-request; a bill at
  approve (set once, the guard refuses a later change). Rows without a snapshot (older, or no approved rate) read the
  rate in force on the confirm / approve day, else today.
- **Departure costs priced by sold seats**, the `app.b2b.departures` rule (confirmed holds, line not declined /
  cancelled). 0 sold uses the smallest band (pax 1). Re-pricing runs as a watermark job (like `bk_price_recompute`),
  not inside the hold confirm / cancel transactions: those paths stay untouched and a hold change re-prices within one
  worker pass. A rate cost whose band has no rate any more keeps its last price.
- **Freeze at request**: a DB trigger freezes the cost when its departure-level booking moves to `requested` (the
  price the supplier was asked for), whoever requests it (hand or purchase order).
- **Departure-level supplier bookings** reuse `supplier_bookings` (`departure_id`, exactly one of case / departure) so
  orders, answers and bills work the same; they move no case status and lock the departure row instead of a case.
- **Purchase orders**: one draft per supplier + currency + service month; Create orders joins an existing draft of
  that group. Send fixes the snapshot and queues `bk_supplier_order_send`; the job requests the pending lines with the
  sender as requester. Outside prod the e-mail goes to `STAFF_NOTIFY_EMAIL` (like agency reminders). "Confirmed" and
  "partly confirmed" are derived from the lines, never stored. A b2c case (kept history) is never ordered.
- **Profit permission** `profit.read` = admin + finance (proposed default; sales and contracting see margins at price
  time through `pricing.read`, not realised profit). Profit routes do not also require `pricing.read` (finance does
  not have it); costs and orders do.
- **Revenue** = the confirmed quote total (agency tier discount already inside the B2B price), or the cancellation fee
  once cancelled; USD at the quote's locked rate (1 for USD). Cost = billed, else planned while live, else the quoted
  net while no supplier booking exists (`cost_basis` says which).

## Changed (vs the issue text)

- Order statuses stored: `draft`, `sending`, `sent`, `cancelled` (the issue listed confirmed / partly_confirmed as
  statuses; they are derived instead, so they can never disagree with the lines).
- `v_departure_cost_lines` added as a helper view between the costs and the share.
- Staff UI is one page `/finance` "Costs & profit" (tabs Profit, Departure costs, Purchase orders) plus USD and the
  departure share on the case cost panel, instead of separate Costs tabs on case and departure pages (there is no
  staff departure page yet; the departure drawer lives on the finance page).
- English UI only for now (the Booking admin has no VI strings yet; `pages-default-english`).

## Tradeoffs

- Watermark job vs re-pricing inside the hold transactions: a short window where a cost shows the old band, against
  not touching the B2B confirm path (contract `booking.b2b.v1`) and its lock order.
- Views compute everything on read (no materialised profit): fine at Dev volume (tens of cases); revisit with real
  traffic (`pg_stat_statements` weekly check).
- The send job is at-least-once like the other mail jobs: a worker dying after SES accepted re-sends the order.
