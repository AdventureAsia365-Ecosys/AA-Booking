# AA-974 (CRM-B2b) — Booking commercial rules: VND, cancellation fees, extend once, re-hold limit

Linear AA-974 ("**VND** selling/quote currency + FX rows; **cancellation-fee policy model** + `cancel` with fee + dry
run; `extend` **once per hold**; **re-hold limit** (429). R: a second extend → 409 `extend_limit`; a 4th re-hold in a
day → 429. E: a VND quote uses the rate in force on the pricing day; cancel inside the fee window returns the scheduled
fee"). Sources: contract `booking.b2b.v1` (root `docs/contracts/booking.b2b.v1.openapi.yaml`), root ADR 0005a
decisions 3 and 4, design v3 §3.4 (Booking-owned additions), §3.5 (deposit link, Fix A3 / A8), §5.2, §6.3 (card
CRM-B2b). Stacked on AA-973 (bk-020). Session S248, agent Claude Code.

## Decisions

- **Scope.** Migration bk-021 (`cancellation_policies` + approvals + a seeded default, `b2b_cancellations`, the extend
  columns and `cancellation_policy_id` on `b2b_holds`, two settings rows) and the routes the contract already names:
  `POST /holds/{id}/extend`, `POST /bookings/{id}/cancel` (+ `?dry_run=true`), the 429 `rehold_limit` on
  `POST /holds`, VND on `POST /quotes`. Staff admin of the fee policies under `/booking/pricing/cancellation-policies`
  (same maker-checker as pricing rules and FX rows). The event feed (`hold.extended`, `booking.cancelled`) is AA-975.

### VND

- **B2B quotes only** (ADR 0005a decision 3: "first for B2B quotes; B2C may enable it later as policy data"). The B2B
  quote currencies are the engine's five selling currencies plus VND (`app.b2b.quotes.QUOTE_CURRENCIES`); the AA-810
  engine's `SELLING_CURRENCIES` (B2C quote-preview, version pricing) is unchanged, so staff B2C screens do not offer
  VND yet.
- **FX**: nothing new in the table: `booking.fx_rates` already takes any ISO code but USD, and the quote already reads
  the approved row in force on the pricing day (`app.pricing.repository.fx_rates_in_force`, newest approved `as_of`
  <= the UTC quote day). "FX rows" for VND are data entered on `/booking/pricing/fx-rates` (maker-checker); Dev uses
  synthetic rates (design §3.5). The VND rate source is AA-930's decision.
- **Rounding (proposed default for Ms. Thư)**: each VND line's `b2b_price` is rounded **up to 1,000 VND** (ADR 0005a
  proposed "to the nearest 1,000 VND"; up, like the cent rule of the other currencies, so rounding never lowers the
  margin); the total is the exact sum of the lines, so it is a multiple of 1,000 too. The rounding rule is part of the
  hash (`line_ceil_1000` for VND, `line_ceil_0.01` otherwise: existing USD / EUR / ... hashes do not change). Amounts
  are written without decimals (contract `Money`: "VND amounts have no decimals").

### Cancellation fees

- **Model** (ADR 0005a decision 4: "a fee schedule by days before departure, per product or contract"):
  `booking.cancellation_policies`, one row per (channel `b2b`, trip plan or NULL = the channel default, `valid_from`)
  with `tiers` = `[{min_days, fee_pct}]`. Cancelling `d` days before departure costs the tier with the largest
  `min_days <= max(d, 0)`; a 0-day tier is required, so a cancel on or after the start day is always priced. The fee
  never falls as the departure gets closer (validated in the app; the DB CHECK guards shape and ranges only).
- **JSONB tiers, not a child table.** A policy row is immutable (append-only like `pricing_rules`); its schedule is part
  of that one fact, read whole, never queried tier by tier. A CHECK with `jsonb_path_exists` guards the shape (array of
  1–20 objects with numeric `min_days` 0–730 incl. 0 and `fee_pct` 0–100).
- **Maker-checker like pricing rules and FX rows** (ADR 0005 d8, UI spec Q19): a new row is pending until a second
  person approves it (`policy_approvals.cancellation_policy_id`, entity `cancellation_policy`). bk-021 adds its own
  BEFORE INSERT trigger function for the new entity rather than replacing bk-007's `policy_approval_maker_checker()`
  (so a replay of bk-007, as the integration tests do, cannot drop the new branch). Staff routes:
  `GET|POST /booking/pricing/cancellation-policies`, `POST …/{id}/approve` (`pricing.read` / `pricing.write`; the
  shared approve code is `app.pricing.policy.approve_row`).
- **Which policy a booking uses**: the plan of the booking's **first `tp:` line** (its tour), else the default; the one
  **in force on the confirm day** is locked on the hold (`b2b_holds.cancellation_policy_id`). A policy approved later
  never changes the fee of an existing booking (commercially, the terms agreed at booking apply). A hold confirmed
  without a locked policy (before this code) resolves the one in force on its confirm day.
- **Fee arithmetic**: days before = trip case `start_date` − the UTC day of the cancel; fee = the quoted
  `total_b2b_price` × % / 100, **half-up to the currency unit** (cent; whole VND); refundable = total − fee (CHECK
  fee + refundable = total). In the quote currency, never re-converted (R17 "stored amounts are never re-converted").
- **AA cancelled the departure → no fee.** If a departure of the booking has status `cancelled` (staff), the cancel
  charges 0 % whatever `reason_code` the agency sends (the reason is the CRM's claim; the departure status is
  Booking's fact). `fee_basis` still names the booking's policy, with `fee_pct` 0. Proposed default for Ms. Thư.
- **Default schedule (proposed default for Ms. Thư)**, seeded and approved as `seed:bk-021`: 60+ days 0 %, 30–59 days
  25 %, 15–29 days 50 %, 7–14 days 75 %, 0–6 days (and after the start) 100 %. A change is a new row + a second
  person's approval on `/booking/pricing/cancellation-policies`, not a migration.
- **Cancel** (`POST /bookings/{booking_id}/cancel`, scope `book`, `Idempotency-Key`): `booking_id` = the trip case id
  (as `Booking.booking_id`). In one transaction under a lock of the hold row: insert `b2b_cancellations`, hold lines →
  `cancelled` (seats are no longer sold: `sold` counts only non-cancelled lines, so the departure gets them back at
  once), trip case `confirmed → cancelled` (bk-005 edge; status note = client, reason, fee; `changed_by` NULL as for
  the confirm), audit `b2b_booking.cancelled`. **Dry run** (`?dry_run=true`) computes the same result and writes
  nothing. A repeat (any key, dry run or not) returns the stored cancellation with `dry_run: false` and its
  `cancelled_at` (the cancel is idempotent on the booking, like confirm). Another partner's booking or an unknown id
  is 404 `not_found`; a booking that is not confirmed is 422 `invalid`.
- **The hold stays `confirmed`** after a cancel. The contract's `Hold.status` has no `cancelled`, and the one-set-per-
  version index keeps the cancelled version blocked (a rebooking is a new proposal version), which is what the CRM
  does anyway. The lines carry `cancelled`.

### Extend once

- `POST /holds/{id}/extend` (scope `hold`, `Idempotency-Key`, `reason` `payment_claimed | client_recheck`): new expiry
  = current expiry + setting `b2b_hold_extend_hours` (**72**, design "proposed +72 h"; proposed default for Ms. Thư),
  capped by the earliest departure start (00:00 UTC), as a place caps it.
- **Once**: `extended_count` 0 → 1 with `extended_at`, `extend_reason`, `extend_idempotency_key` (CHECK ties them).
  A retry with the same key returns the hold (200), so a CRM retry after a timeout is safe; any other extend of an
  extended hold is **409 `extend_limit`** (Linear R). An extend that cannot move the expiry (the hold already runs to
  the departure start) is also 409 `extend_limit`, and does not use up the extension.
- Expired / released → 410 `hold_expired` (re-hold); confirmed → 422 `invalid` (nothing to extend).
- **Race with a place**: like confirm, extend locks the hold, then its departures (id order), and judges expiry on
  `clock_timestamp()` read after the locks. A place that started after the old expiry and counted these seats as free
  holds the departure lock; the extend waits, then sees the hold expired → 410. Without this, an extend could revive a
  hold whose seats a newer hold already took.

### Re-hold limit

- **Per partner and proposal ref**, as the design says (§3.4 "Per tenant and per proposal reference: re-holds ≤
  3/day, 429 `rehold_limit`"; §3.5 "Re-checks from C-12: ≤ 3 a day per proposal"). The lane brief paraphrased it as
  "the same departure by the same agency"; the design row is the source, and the abuse it stops is the anonymous
  client link re-checking one proposal (Fix A3). Departure-level abuse is already bounded by capacity.
- **What a re-hold is**: any hold of a proposal ref after its first hold ever, whatever its version. Re-holds per UTC
  day ≤ setting `b2b_rehold_limit_per_day` (**3**); the next is **429 `rehold_limit`** with `retry_after_s` = seconds
  to 00:00 UTC. So on the day of the first hold, holds 2–4 pass and the 5th (the 4th re-hold) is refused (Linear R).
- Idempotent answers never count and are never refused: a replay of the same key, or the same quote for a version
  that already holds, is answered before the check.
- **Concurrency**: a transaction advisory lock on `hashtextextended(partner || ':' || proposal_ref, 974)` serialises
  the holds of one ref, so two re-holds at the limit cannot both pass the count (integration race test).
- The UTC day is used because Booking has no agency time zone (proposed default for Ms. Thư).

## Changed

- Linear card wording vs implementation: "4th re-hold in a day → 429" is implemented per proposal ref (see above);
  `extend_limit` 409 also covers "cannot move the expiry".
- `app.pricing.policy._approve` became `approve_row` (public, a `NOT_FOUND` map) so the cancellation policies reuse
  the same maker-checker code; pricing rules and FX rows behave exactly as before.
- `app.b2b.holds._recheck_under_lock` returns the locked departure rows (extend caps the new expiry with them).
- The AA-973 test cleanup (`tests/integration/test_bk020_b2b.py`) also deletes `b2b_cancellations` and plan-specific
  cancellation policies (new FKs to holds, cases and plans).
- Root contract `booking.b2b.v1` (separate root PR, additive): extend lists 404 / 422 and its retry rule, cancel lists
  404 / 422 and its idempotency, quotes say how VND is rounded.

## Tradeoffs

- **Policy locked on the hold vs read at cancel time.** Locking costs one column and one query at confirm, and
  protects the agency from a policy changed after booking; reading at cancel time would be simpler but would let a
  new policy re-price old bookings.
- **One policy per booking (the first tour line) vs per line.** The contract's `fee_basis` names one policy and one
  fee %, and a B2B booking is one tour plus extras. Per-line policies would need a per-line fee breakdown the
  contract does not have.
- **Fee on the whole quoted total**, including on-request lines that AA may still decline: a declined line is a
  later AA-975 event; until then the agency pays by the schedule on what it booked. Flag for Ms. Thư.
- **VND rounding up to 1,000 per line** gives a total that is a multiple of 1,000 and never lowers the margin; the
  cost is at most 999 VND (≈ 0.04 USD) per line.
- **No `hold.extended` / `booking.cancelled` events yet**: the `b2b_event` outbox is AA-975; the audit rows and the
  stored cancellation are the record until then.
