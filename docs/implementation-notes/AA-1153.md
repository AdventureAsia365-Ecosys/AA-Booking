# AA-1153 part 1: hotel contracts through DMCs (Jira PR-30)

Scope of part 1 (root `docs/audits/2026-11-19-S285-session/advisory-r2-plan.md` slice L10): T8 property master, T9
board basis, B7 net prices, T10 DMC rate-sheet importer with business checks and a diff before approval, contract
seasons and per-night prices, external codes, T11 hotel confirmation number and the facts re-confirmation date. Part 2:
allotment / release days on quotes (B8) and the Excel two-way round trip (facts and room-type sheets, export).

Migration `bk-069` (contract class) ships first in its own PR; the code PR raises readiness to `bk-069`.

## Decisions

- **`hotels.supplier_id` stays** (NOT NULL, "the supplier that first listed the hotel"). The DMCs that sell a hotel are
  `booking.hotel_supplier_links` rows (backfilled one per hotel; an AFTER INSERT trigger keeps it for every new hotel).
  Making `supplier_id` nullable and dropping the old unique key is a later contract step, once nothing reads it.
  Every check "is this room type the supplier's?" now asks for an active link: the two DB guards (rates,
  restrictions), `rates.create_rate`, `restrictions._component`, the supplier-booking supply check and the importer.
- **Which DMC's price a quote takes** (`app.supply.rates`, one SQL for every caller): per supplier, the same choice as
  before (newest validity, pax band, lowest net), with the asked board first; across suppliers the asked board first,
  then the hotel's preferred DMC (`is_preferred`), then the lowest net **in USD** (review fix: each candidate's net
  at the approved FX rate in force on the night, the quote's own FX source; a currency without an approved rate sorts
  after the others, then the raw net). A service has one supplier, so services price exactly as before.
- **One DMC per room line** (review fix): `components._one_dmc_per_room` takes the DMC of a room's first unit (first
  night, first occupancy of the mix) for every unit of that room type on the line (`rt:` line, or the nights of one
  room type of a `tp:` line) and asks the other units again pinned to it in one more batched query (only when a unit
  went to another DMC). A unit the DMC cannot price refuses the line (422 naming "the DMC that prices the first night");
  the staff version path already did this (`default_room_costs`). Chosen over "the DMC with the lowest stay total":
  same rule on both paths, and a total needs every DMC's rate for every unit (N x DMCs lookups).
- **Board basis (T9)**: a lookup asks a board or none. None = the pricing policy's default board
  (`hotel_default_board`, review fix: a policy parameter of a new single-choice type `code`, choices RO/BB/HB/FB/AI,
  seeded BB by bk-069, approved by the pricing group, audited like every parameter): an exact rate on it first, then a
  rate with no board, then any other board (old data keeps pricing). An explicit board takes that board or a rate with
  no board, never another board (a BB rate never silently wins over an asked HB). The board comes from the quote line
  (`meal_plan`, agency or staff desk), else the tour night (`trip_plan_components.meal_plan`), else the policy default.
  The SQL takes it as a bind parameter ($8). A B2B quote reads it from its own policy load (the quote day), the
  tariff once per build, the cost sheet in `load_pricing`, staff paths (version defaults, rate-in-force) today's value
  (503 `policy_unavailable` without one). `price_hash` is unchanged while it stays BB; changing it re-prices quotes made
  afterwards (as any rate change does).
- **Double dinner warning**: a tour line whose night is HB / FB / AI and whose same day holds a `meal` service, or an
  `rt:` line on HB / FB / AI with a `meal` service line of the same quote on one of its nights, gets
  `price_note.board.warnings` (`double_dinner` with the dates). A warning only; the price is unchanged.
- **The booking goes to the DMC of the price used**: a quote part stores the supplier of its rate's contract (was the
  hotel's supplier); a version item's supplier is the contract supplier of its first cost row's rate, else of the B2B
  quote line's first rate, else the hotel's / service's.
- **B7** was already in bk-060 (`published_amount` + `commission_pct`, net derived half-up). Part 1 adds nothing but
  the end-to-end proof (a second DMC's "70 USD, 20 %" row imports as 56.00 with both source numbers).
- **Hotel child prices** are per (contract, hotel), age band, basis sharing / extra bed, optional board, net per night.
  A room line charges them only for the children the room mix cannot hold (rooms given explicitly; the default mix
  already rooms every traveller): the oldest children first (conservative), each by the band of its age
  (`child_ages`), an unknown age by the band reaching the oldest age. No matching policy = no extra (as before) and
  `price_note.child_room` says so. Review fix: the basis is chosen on purpose (`components.child_bases`): `sharing`
  while the rooms' free places (`max_occupancy` minus each room's occupancy, over the mix; all children when the room
  type has no maximum) hold the child, `extra_bed` for the oldest beyond them; the other basis only when the DMC has no
  price on the chosen one (a priced bed rather than an unpriced child). bk-069's unique key includes `basis`, so a sheet
  may give both prices for one band; `price_note.child_room.extra_bed` counts the children on an extra bed. No request
  field for the basis yet (a contract minor; follow-up if agencies need to ask an extra bed explicitly).
- **Seasons**: `supplier_contract_seasons` (name + range; a season may have several ranges). A rate keeps its own dates
  and season label; per-night pricing reads the rate dates. The importer's optional `Seasons` sheet expands a rate row
  that names a season and gives no dates into one rate per range.
- **Business checks** (`app.supply.rate_checks`, pure): `board_order` (RO <= BB <= HB <= FB <= AI for the same room,
  dates, band), `season_gap` (contract days no season covers), `season_overlap` (two seasons on one day: an error),
  `price_jump` (a net above the previous contract's same rate by the policy `rate_sheet_jump_warn_pct`). Errors block
  the import; warnings are listed at the dry run, stored with the import and must be acknowledged by the second person
  who publishes a draft that came from an import (`acknowledge_checks`, 409 `checks_unacknowledged` otherwise).
- **Diff before approval** = the draft's rates against the supplier's previous published contract, keyed by target,
  board, pax type, pax band and season (or the rate dates): added / changed (old and new net, %) / removed. Live on
  `GET /booking/contracts/{id}/review`, stored at import time in `rate_sheet_imports.diff`.
- **Duplicate hotels** (`app.supply.hotel_master`): normalised name (lower case, accents and punctuation removed,
  common words such as hotel / resort / spa dropped), `difflib` ratio, haversine distance. Block: similarity >=
  `hotel_match_block_pct` and (closer than `hotel_match_block_m`, or no coordinates on one side and the same place).
  Warn: similarity >= `hotel_match_warn_pct`, or the same place and closer than `hotel_match_warn_m`: a create or a
  name / place / position change needs `not_duplicate_reason` (10..300 characters, audited). Thresholds are pricing
  policy parameters (section hotels), never literals.
- **Re-confirmation**: `facts_confirmed_on` + the policy `hotel_facts_reconfirm_days` = `facts_reconfirm_due`; the hotel
  list filters `reconfirm=due`. A reminder job is not part 1 (the contract expiry job of AA-1151 is the place to add
  it).

## Changed

- The rate-sheet importer matches hotels linked to the supplier by the DMC's code first, then the AA code; room types
  by name, then by alias (the supplier's alias first, then an any-DMC alias).
- `rate_in_force` (one lookup) now runs the batched query and reads the row by id: one rate choice everywhere.
- A version item's room default net is priced night by night (one cost row per run of nights at one rate), the first
  night choosing the DMC and the next nights staying with it.

- **Rate-sheet bounds** (review fix): the `Seasons` and `Hotel children` rows count against the same `MAX_ROWS` (2000)
  as the rate rows; the season expansion is refused as soon as it passes `MAX_ROWS` rates; the workbook is refused when
  its unpacked size passes `MAX_UNPACKED_BYTES` (40 MB, a technical zip-bomb bound, not a policy value); the overlap
  check is one sweep (each range against the furthest-reaching earlier range of every other season: one error per
  range, no pairwise loop). Duplicate season ranges and duplicate child prices (hotel, board, basis, band start) are
  row problems at parse time; a database refusal of a season or child row names its own sheet and row.
- **Rebase on AA-1158 part 2 + AA-1151** (S289): readiness lists bk-067, bk-068 and bk-069 (readiness = bk-069); the
  quote rate lookup returns the add-on offer child % (AA-1151) and the DMC-pinned units side by side. To keep
  `b2b/quotes.py` and `suppliers/service.py` under the 800-line file cap, the quote line's board hash and price-note
  parts moved to `app.b2b.components` (`board_hash`, `LineCost.notes`) and the publish acknowledgement of an imported
  draft to `app.supply.rate_review.require_acknowledged`.

## Tradeoffs

- No `pg_trgm`: `difflib` over the hotels of the same place / country / bounding box is enough at today's size
  (hundreds of hotels) and needs no extension migration.
- Child prices only for children beyond the room mix: a triple room for two adults and a child is the triple rate,
  as DMC sheets price it.
- Warnings never block the import itself (DMC promotions are real); they block an unacknowledged publish.

  bk-068, bk-069 as one Booking batch.
- `booking.b2b.v1` gains two optional request fields on a quote line (`meal_plan`, `child_ages`): contract minor 1.10
  in the root PR. They are hashed only when sent, so an existing request hashes as before.

- Left for later (follow-ups, not part 1): the can-operate check (N3 "supplier not active") and the B2B desk labels
  still name `hotels.supplier_id` (the first DMC), not every linked DMC; the agency voucher (`booking.b2b.v1`) does not
  print `hotel_confirmation_ref` yet (a contract minor); no reminder job for facts due again (list filter only); the
  cost sheet (price matrix) does not add hotel child prices (it prices adults per occupancy, as before); AA-CIS-Infra

# AA-1153 live-check support — the E2E pricing maker's hotel link and desk quote (S289, root ADR 0006a A10e-5)

## Decisions

- **The quote route of the Done-when is the staff desk entry** (POST `/booking/b2b-desk/entries`): the agency API
  `POST /booking/b2b/v1/quotes` takes an M2M agency token, which no staff E2E identity holds; the desk entry runs the
  same `quotes.quote_in` (then hold and confirm) for a chosen agency. The quote line keeps `meal_plan` and `child_ages`.
- **Two writes for the A10e-2 pricing maker**, both on synthetic supply only: (a) POST `/booking/hotels/{ref}/suppliers`
  (body `supplier_id`, optional `supplier_hotel_code`, `is_preferred`): the hotel from the path and the DMC from the body
  are both the maker's synthetic supply; (b) the desk entry: the agency is synthetic in CIS, the agency reference
  carries the `SYNTHETIC` mark, no price override, room lines `rt:` only (no `tp:` / service line, no departure, no
  room's own room type), every room type on a synthetic hotel all of whose DMC links (any status) are synthetic, so no
  real DMC's rate prices it and no real DMC is booked. One SQL for the agency and the rooms.
- **Route-scoped grant**: the desk entry needs `b2b_desk.book` (admin, sales); the maker is `contracting`, so that one
  request holds it after every check passed (the A10e-1 mechanism). The snapshot test now allows a grant on a write
  whose body is checked (not only fixed).
- **File cap**: the body checks moved to `app/auth/e2e_staff_bodies.py` (leaf module), the record checks to
  `app/auth/e2e_staff_supply.py`; `e2e_staff.py` keeps the allow-list (748 lines).

## Should know

- Root ADR 0006a A10e-5 records the widening (root PR first); it is temporary like the whole A10 family (AA-1054).
- Live check: maker links the second synthetic DMC (201), the checker publishes its HB rate sheet contract, the maker
  enters the desk booking (3 nights HB, 2 adults + a 7-year-old) on the synthetic agency; the quote line's
  `net_cost_usd` matches the cost.


# AA-1153 part 2: allotment on B2B room lines, two-way rate sheet, desk DMC label (S290)

Scope: B8 (allotment / free sale / on request / release days for hotel room lines in `booking.supply_restrictions`),
T10 two-way Excel (export the contract, edit, import back with the dry-run diff and the import rules), and the part 1
follow-up "the B2B desk on-request list names the hotel's first DMC". Migration `bk-072` (contract class: two pricing
policy rows) ships first in its own PR; the code PR raises readiness to `bk-072`.

## Decisions

- **Terms are per DMC.** A quote's supply ask now names the supplier of the rate that priced it
  (`SupplyAsk.supplier_id`, from `components._supply_ask`); the engine reads only that supplier's rows (its allotment,
  free-sale, on-request terms and its stop-sales) and counts only that supplier's use. A stop-sale of DMC A no longer
  closes the room for a line DMC B prices (each DMC's sale to AA is its own; every term "is the term the supplier gives
  AA"). With one DMC per hotel (all data before part 1) nothing changes. The staff check without `supplier_id` keeps
  reading every supplier's rows (as before).
- **Which lines are lifted.** Only `rt:` room lines: their departure answer is always `on_request` (no departure), and
  every night and occupancy unit available from the engine makes the line `available`. `tp:` lines keep the departure
  answer (a stop-sale still puts them on request) and `sv:` lines stay on request: a service allotment on quotes is a
  later slice (same engine, the hold re-check would need the service seats).
- **Allotment use** = supplier bookings requested / confirmed (`quantity` rooms, at least 1; was "one room per
  booking") + rooms of live B2B `rt:` lines held `available` (active and not expired, or confirmed and the line not
  cancelled / declined; the room count from the quote line's `rooms`, else the default mix `(party + 1) // 2`) +, inside
  one call, the earlier asks an allotment answered (two lines of one quote share the allotment). A supplier booking of
  a version item whose B2B hold line was held available is not counted (the hold line counts it with its real rooms).
  The quote leaves out its own proposal-version hold, as the departure seats do.
- **The hold re-checks** (`app/b2b/room_hold.py`): a room line the quote answered available is checked again under a
  transaction advisory lock per room type (`hashtextextended('booking.room_allotment:' || id)`, id order), so two
  agencies cannot both take the last room; still covered = held `available` (the confirm confirms it at once, no desk
  line), else held `on_request`. The AA-1091 Q9 rule (a quote's on-request line is held on request) moved into the
  same module; `holds.py` shrank (code-health baseline file).
- **Release days default** = pricing policy `allotment_release_days_default` (bk-072, seeded 7, section hotels,
  approver pricing), applied when a term is written without release days (form or Inventory sheet) and stored on the
  row: changing the policy later does not move existing terms (a contract term is fixed once published).
- **Row cap** = pricing policy `rate_sheet_max_rows` (bk-072, seeded 2000 = the old constant). `parse(data, max_rows)`
  takes it (None = no cap, used by tests and parsing outside an import); the import reads the value in force today.
  The export refuses a contract with more rows than the cap (422 `rate_sheet_too_many_rows`) so an export always
  imports back. The byte bounds (2 MB, 40 MB unpacked) stay technical constants.
- **Export** writes the import template exactly: Contract block, Hotel / service sheets (Net plus the B7 Published and
  Commission % when the rate came from them; the importer accepts a Net equal to the derived net), Seasons, Hotel
  children, Inventory. A hotel is written with the DMC's own code for it when the link has one (the importer matches
  that code first), else its AA code; a room type by its AA name.
- **The diff of a re-import** is against the supplier's published contract starting on or before the draft
  (`rate_review.PREVIOUS_SQL`: `<=`, was `<`): an exported, edited contract starting the same day is the one it
  replaces. The draft itself stays excluded.
- **Desk label**: `desk.LINE_FROM` joins the supplier of the quote line's first rate (rooms and services; a `tp:` line
  has many suppliers and keeps none); `line_supplier` falls back to the component's supplier. The list and the line
  detail use it.
- **Supply options of a DMC** (`GET /booking/supply/options?supplier_id=`) also list the room types of hotels the
  supplier is linked to (part 1 gap: the rate and term drawers of DMC B found no room).

## Changed

- `restrictions.py` lost its reads (`RESTRICTIONS_SQL`, usage SQL, `availability_many`, `check`) to
  `app/supply/availability.py` (`many`, `check`, `line_availability`, `room_line`); `quotes.supply_availability` moved
  there as `line_availability` (file caps: `quotes.py` 797 lines, `restrictions.py` gains the policy default).
- `rate_sheet.py` (789 lines) split: cell helpers, `Problem`, `_table` and the row `Budget` moved to
  `rate_sheet_cells.py` (re-exported from `rate_sheet`, so `activity_import` keeps reading them there).
- Staff UI (supplier › Rates): an "Excel" button exports the selected contract; a "Room and service inventory" section
  lists the contract's terms (release date of the first night shown) with Add term / Remove while the contract is a
  draft; the import drawer lists the Inventory terms of a dry run and says when the policy default fills release days.

- **Review fix: one contract's terms per DMC and date.** An exported, edited contract imports back as a new draft
  with a copy of every Inventory term; published, it sits next to the contract it replaces (publishing never expires
  the older one). The engine (`restrictions.current_terms`, run inside `evaluate`) keeps, per supplier, only the
  allotment / free-sale / on-request rows of the newest published contract (`published_at`, then contract id) among
  those with a term on the date; stop-sales all count. Allotment 2 + re-imported allotment 2 = 2 rooms, not 4. Chosen
  over expiring the previous contract at publish: `rate_sheet_imports.previous_contract_id` is the contract starting
  on or before the draft, which may still be in force for earlier dates (a 2027 sheet must not expire 2026), and
  expiring is a separate approval. To end an older term early on some dates, the new contract carries an
  `on_request` term on them (or the older contract is expired).
- **Review fix: export text never becomes a formula.** `rate_sheet.build_workbook` writes each row through `_append`:
  a str starting with `app.b2b.tariff.FORMULA_START` (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab, CR) is a string cell with Excel's
  quote prefix, value unchanged (as `app.catalog.price_import._as_text`; that helper cannot be imported here,
  `price_import` imports `rate_sheet`). Titles, notes, season and room names come from DMC sheets (third-party text).

## Tradeoffs

- Newest published contract per DMC and date (not per contract validity): a re-import that drops a term leaves the
  older contract's term counting on its dates until that contract expires or the new one writes an `on_request` term
  there. Rates keep their own choice (`rates.RATE_ORDER`: newest rate validity, then the lowest net): two published
  contracts of one DMC with the same validity price from the lower net (follow-up, not part of this fix).
- A per-room-type advisory lock instead of a row lock: there is no inventory row per night to lock (terms are ranges);
  the lock is held only for the hold's transaction.
- The room count of a held line is recomputed in SQL from the stored request (`rooms` or the default mix), not stored:
  no migration on `b2b_quote_lines` / `b2b_hold_lines` (a contract-class ALTER and a MIG-4 chain for one number).
- Price notes list at most 10 dates per reason (`MAX_NOTE_DATES`, the 2000-byte note CHECK); stop-sale dates stay
  complete as before.
- Free sale without a limit lifts any party (`max_pax` empty), as the bk-060 engine already answered.

## Should know

  `bk-072`). The running code ignores both keys until then.
- An allotment does not reserve anything at quote time; the hold is the reservation. A quote can still say `available`
  and its hold `on_request` when another agency held the last rooms in between (by design, like departures).
- Local integration: the 15 failures of `test_bk045_schema` / `test_bk069_schema` in one long local run happen on
  `origin/main` too (order of the files on one database); both files pass alone.
- Follow-ups (not this slice): service allotments on `sv:` lines (and their hold re-check); the agency voucher
  `hotel_confirmation_ref`; a reminder job for hotel facts due again; the Rates tab still shows the net only (no
  Published / Commission % column; the API and the export have both).
