# AA-1177 — Supplier contract expiry reminders reach the admins

Lane f-contract-expiry of S290. Business owner's ask (Jira PR-31, line added 24/11): supplier prices hold for a period
(about one year) and the system must remind the admins before they end. Builds on AA-1151 (bk-068): the daily job
`bk_contract_expiry_watch`, the window `contract_expiry_alert_days` (60) and the in-app notification
`contract.expiring`. Migration bk-074 (migration-only PR first, MIG-4), then this code PR.

## Decisions

- **E-mail path: Booking's own SES sender (`app.mailer`)**, the one the agency invoice reminders and the purchase
  orders already use (AA-811, `SES_FROM_ADDRESS`, `ses:FromAddress` IAM condition). No new mail path, no new env var,
  no Infra change: the Booking task role may already send as the shared no-reply address.
- **Recipients are a policy value, resolved at send time.** `contract_expiry_mail_roles` (codes from the built-in staff
  roles; Pricing > Policy, section operations, approved by the pricing group like the window; seeded
  `["admin", "contracting"]`). The job reads the active `booking.staff_users` of those roles with an address. No
  recipient list in code, env or a table; an empty list switches the e-mail off.
- **One message per person**, never one message with every staff address in To (nobody sees colleagues' addresses).
  Deduplicated by address.
- **The mail record lives on the announcement row** (`booking.notifications.data.mail`): the announcement is already
  unique per contract and end date (`notifications_reminder_uq` on `reminder_key`), so "the same reminder_key" gives
  "once per contract and end date" without a new table, a new retention row or a second idempotency key. It keeps
  staff keys (`sent`, `refused`), never addresses.
- **Every environment mails the real staff of the roles** (unlike the agency reminders, which redirect to the staff
  inbox outside prod: agencies are external, staff are not). Outside prod the subject starts with the environment.
  On Dev, SES may refuse an address it cannot deliver to (sandbox): that address is recorded `refused` and never
  retried, the others are delivered.
- **Body EN then VI in one message** (staff pick no mail language today); business facts only.
- **My work tile `contracts_ending_soon` needs `supply.write`** (admin, contracting: the people who renew contracts);
  sales and finance still see the lists through `supply.read`.
- **Where the list shows**: Suppliers (panel above the table; the tile opens `/suppliers?contracts=ending`, which scrolls
  to it and shows it even when empty), a supplier's Contracts tab (that supplier only, `?supplier=`), Add-ons (as
  before). One shared component `suppliers/_parts/ExpiringContracts.tsx`.

## Changed

- `GET /booking/contracts/expiring`: new optional `supplier` (UUID or code); each item adds `supplier_categories`
  (the contract kind), `mailed`, plus the existing `reference` / `supplier_id` now typed in the frontend.
- The Suppliers KPI "Contracts ending in 30 days" (a fixed 30 in `suppliers/service.py` summary) is replaced on the page
  by "Contracts ending within N days" from the policy window (lesson S289: a UI threshold is a policy value). The API
  field `contracts_ending_30d` stays (removing it is a breaking OpenAPI change): follow-up.
- The guide text of `contract_expiry_alert_days` now names the e-mail, the tile and the pages.
- Job handler: `contract_expiry.HANDLERS` became `contract_expiry.build_handler(settings)` (it needs the mail
  settings); the kind name and schedule are unchanged.

## Tradeoffs

- **Notification data vs a new table**: a table (`contract_expiry_mail`, one row per person) would give per-person
  history and an index, but needs a migration with DDL, a DM-7.1 retention row and a root PR. The jsonb record is
  enough for "once per contract and end date" and lives and goes with its notification row.
- **Someone given the role after a contract was mailed does not get that contract's mail** (the record is done). The
  in-app notification and the lists still show it. Mailing late joiners would need per-person rows (above).
- **Nobody holds a mail role / no SES**: nothing is recorded, so the contracts still in the window are mailed once
  someone does / mail is configured; a contract that left the window meanwhile is not mailed.
