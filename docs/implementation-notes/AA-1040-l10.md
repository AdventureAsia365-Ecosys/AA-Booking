# AA-1040 (lane l10) — the AI mark on the CIS supply text Booking shows

S266, EU AI Act Art. 50, root ADR 0019 d19. The AI Use Register named "Booking hotel and service descriptions drafted
by `supply_content_writer`" as a surface with no machine mark on the Booking side.

## Decisions

- **Storage is CIS's, not Booking's.** Booking never stores the CIS text (ADR 0002 / 0004a: read live from
  `catalog.v_supply_content`). The mark comes from CIS too: CIS 366 (AA-CIS-App, contract migration) appends
  `ai_generated`, `ai_generated_at`, `ai_human_edited`, derived from `shared.supply_content_version.origin` and the
  `based_on_version_id` chain. So no `bk-` migration.
- **Detected on its own.** `MARK_VIEW` lists only the three new columns; the read-back's nine columns keep their own
  detection, so the content keeps working before 366 (`ai: null`) and the mark turns on by itself after it.
  `app/catalog/liveness.py` now keys its cache by the `View` (schema, name, columns) instead of the view name, so two
  detections of one view never share an answer.
- **Shape** `{ai_generated, generator: "Adventure Asia supply content writer", model: null, generated_at,
  human_edited}`, the CIS tour mark's shape. `model` is null: CIS records it on its ledger only.
- **UI**: the kit `AiNotice form="badge"` ("AI-assisted", staff sentence as tooltip / accessible name) and `data-ai-*`
  attributes on the text block, in the hotel / service CIS panel, the service form's CIS description, the proposal
  builder line and the proposal preview. The badge shows on any CIS text unless `ai_generated` is false (a person wrote
  it from scratch in CIS): every published supply text starts as a writer draft, so a null mark (before 366) still
  gets the badge. Helpers in `ui/booking/aiMark.ts` (node tests); `AiNotice` exported from the `@/ui` barrel.

## Changed

- Backend: `app/supply/content.py` (`MARK_VIEW`, `ai_mark`, `content.ai`, `supply.ai`), `app/catalog/liveness.py`
  (cache key), `tests/unit/test_aa1040_supply_ai_mark.py`, `tests/unit/test_supply_content.py`,
  `tests/integration/test_bk023_media_api.py` (the stand-in gains the three columns at the end).
- Frontend: `ui/booking/aiMark.ts` + test, `ui/index.ts`, `lib/api/supply.ts`, `lib/api/types.ts`,
  `app/(staff)/_parts/SupplyContent.tsx`, `services/_parts/ServiceFormDrawer.tsx`, the proposal builder and preview,
  `e2e/layout/fixtures.ts` (the panel fixture carries a mark).

## Tradeoffs

- Badge on an unknown mark rather than hiding it: over-labelling CIS text is harmless, missing a label is not.
