# AA-1148 — Supplier category `dmc` and the supplier list for the CIS content workspace (Booking side)

Lane b-content-workspace of session S290. The CIS content workspace (AA-CIS-App) creates tours by hand and links each
source tour to the AA supplier it comes from; the supplier list is Booking's. Two Booking PRs:

1. **Migration only, bk-073** (contract, MIG-4): `suppliers_categories_chk` allows `dmc` next to the six service
   categories (1 to 7 of them); the view `booking.v_supply_suppliers` (contract `supply.supplier.v1`: id, code,
   company_name, categories, country_code, status, updated_at) with SELECT to `booking` and `cis_app`.
2. **Code** (this PR, chain: booking/073 applied first): the supplier API and forms accept `dmc`; readiness requires
   bk-073.

## Decisions

- **Named `dmc`, not `tour`** (S285 advisory T5): AA still buys a program component by component (B12); `dmc` says who
  the supplier is (a destination management company that runs whole programs), not what is sold as one price. A DMC
  that also sells hotels or activities carries those categories too: the hotel / service / activity rules keep asking
  for their own category (`records.py`, `hotel_master.py`, `activity_import.py` unchanged).
- **Identity only in the view**: no contact, internal notes, legal entity, tax code, payment terms, bank account or
  price. The CIS reader fails soft when the view or the grant is missing.
- The grant to `cis_app` is mirrored in AA-CIS-Infra `db/owners.yaml` (consumer grant, expected from bk-073) and the
  isolation probe; the company name is on the PII allowlist (a business name, no contact person).

## Changed

- Nothing against the advisory. Booking's prefill of `trip_plans.supplier_id` from the CIS value (T5 step 5) is a
  follow-up (it needs the CIS id in `catalog.v_tour`).

## Tradeoffs

- One view of every supplier (pending and inactive included, with their status) rather than active ones only: CIS shows
  what an old tour is linked to even after the supplier is switched off, and picks only active / pending ones.

- `docs/api/openapi.json` regenerated with the pinned FastAPI / pydantic (the `Category` enum gains `dmc`, max 7).
