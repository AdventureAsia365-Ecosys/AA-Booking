# AA-1175: group-size price bands as a pricing policy parameter

The cost sheet priced every computed matrix on bands fixed in code (`PAX_BANDS` = 1, 2, 3–4, 5–6, 7–10, 11+). They
are now the parameter `price_pax_bands` of Pricing > Policy (versioned, two-person approval, audited, EN + VI guide),
read for the recompute day. Migration `bk-066` (own PR, MIG-4) seeds it.

## Decisions

- **D1 Seeded bands 1, 2, 3, 4, 5–7, 8–10, 11+ (7 bands).** The business owner asked for 1, 2, 4, 5–7, 8–10.
  Bands must be contiguous from 1, so 3 needs a band. 3 on its own keeps her "4 pax" band exact: a 3–4 band is priced
  for 3 travellers (a band is priced at its smallest party) and would charge a party of 4 more than her 1770. The top
  band is open from 11 (the old code also had an open 11+), priced for 11, so it never undercuts 8–10 (the price per
  person falls as the party grows). Fewer rows was the alternative (1, 2, 3–4, 5–7, 8–10, 11+), rejected for the 4-pax
  reason.
- **D2 Storage.** Key `price_pax_bands`, new type `pax_bands`, section `prices`, approver group `pricing`, unit
  `travellers`, range 1..60 (as `group_on_request_min_pax`), at most 8 bands. Stored as a JSON list of
  `[smallest, largest | null]`. `MAX_BANDS = 8` is a `# technical-constant:` (200 matrix rows / 2 channels / 8 bands =
  12 date windows), not a policy value.
- **D3 Two seeded versions.** v1 = the old code bands from 2026-01-01, v2 = the owner's bands from 2026-11-23,
  replacing v1 with the frozen diff, so the history says what priced until now. Approver text
  `tech lead S288 (...)`, like the earlier seeds.
- **D4 Approval stays two people.** The issue mentions "flagged business-owner self-approval", but every pricing
  parameter uses the maker-checker flow of `policy_admin` with the table CHECK `actor <> maker` (bk-050); the AA-1158
  self-approval switches cover other records. "Like the other parameters" therefore means two people. A
  self-approval for pricing parameters would need its own migration and decision.
- **D5 Bands of the recompute day.** The bands in force on `priced_on` (the day the rules and the rounding are read),
  not per date window. A scheduled version applies from the first recompute on or after its day.
- **D6 Watermark.** `WATERMARK_SQL` also counts the approved pricing policy events, so approving a change in force
  today queues the recompute at the next scheduler tick (it also closes the same gap for the rounding increments). A
  version scheduled for a later day is picked up when the UTC day rolls (already in the watermark).
- **D7 No bands in force** (cannot happen after bk-066; policy rows are never deleted): warning
  `cost_sheet_pax_bands_unavailable`, `Pricing.bands = ()`, no computed row, reason `no_rule` (the readiness report
  says the same through `Pricing.can_price`).
- **D8 No cross-check with `group_on_request_min_pax`** (seed 9). Quotes never read bands; the guide explains that a
  group at or above the on-request size is still quoted on request.
- **D12 The agency quote stays party-exact (review fix).** The issue's scope and Done-when say "the quote calculator
  reads the bands" and "the quote for 4, 6 and 9 pax picks the right band". `POST /b2b/v1/quotes` prices a `tp:` line
  from the plan's components for the real party and was not changed: making it charge the band row (priced for the
  band's smallest group) would raise agency prices for every party above a band's bottom, a product decision for the
  tech lead. What holds instead: the agency tariff row for 4, 6 and 9 is the band that holds the party, and the quote
  prices 4 and 6 at their own cost (6 below the 5-7 band) with 9 on request. The guide (EN and VI) says so; it first
  claimed that a quote uses the band. The Linear Done-when line needs that wording.
- **D9 Workbook.** Import unchanged (hand rows accept any Pax min / Pax max). The template and the prefilled workbook
  add one README line naming the bands in force (`pax_bands.workbook_line`, the router reads the policy).
- **D10 Single supplement whatever the bands.** `asks()` always reads the single and double rate of each room that
  holds two, so a band set without 1 or 2 as its own band still gets the supplement.
- **D11 Season label** from the band that holds 2 travellers (`index_for(bands, 2)`), the only band when bands are
  capped at 1.

## Tradeoffs

- More bands give each group size a fairer price but fewer future date windows fit in the 200-row matrix (7 bands x 2
  channels = 14 rows per window, 14 windows, was 16). The guide says so.
- Prices move once on the first recompute after deploy: unchanged for 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 (5–7 is still priced for 5) and
  11+; 4 gets its own, cheaper row; 7 now pays the 5-traveller price (up, was 7–10 priced for 7); 8–10 pay the
  8-traveller price (down).
- Splitting `policy_shapes.py` and `cost_sheet_sql.py` is churn, but the file-size ratchet forbids growing
  `policy_params.py` (baseline 826) and `cost_sheet.py` must stay under 800 lines.
