# AA-1075 implementation notes: agency booking operations (bk-047)

S277 lane l23 (Claude Code). Scope from the Linear issue and the S275 study
(root `docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md`, items bk-11, bk-12, bk-13 step 1, bk-14,
bk-15, bk-18). Our own code; the OPL-1 app was not read or copied.

## Decisions

- **One transaction for a staff booking.** `quotes.quote_in`, `holds.place_in` and `holds.confirm_in` are the agency
  API's services on one connection, so the staff path gets every API rule (served tours, seats, allotments, credit
  stop, re-hold limit, locked cancellation policy) without a second implementation. The partner's M2M client id goes
  on the quote and hold rows (their CHECKs require one); `holds.Caller.staff_ref` makes the audit rows and the status
  history name the staff member.
- **`booking.created_by_aa` comes from the hold trigger**, not from the new table: the hold is confirmed before its
  lines, so the agency reads the import event before the `line.confirmed` events of the same transaction.
- **Price override = the quote itself.** The lines are scaled to the staff total before hashing, so the hold, the case
  and the settlement all carry the staff price with no special case. Floor = the rule's floor margin, no agency
  discount, per line rounded up; when the agency's approved discount already sells lower, the floor is that price
  (no second person for a price the tier already allows).
- **Below the floor: the hold waits, not the request.** Seats are held at entry (`b2b_hold_hours`); the approver
  confirms the same hold. A hold that expires first reads `lapsed` (no job: computed on read, written on the decision).
- **Maker-checker** for the price override and the cancellation fee override is `b2b_desk.approve` (admin,
  contracting: who own pricing); asking is `b2b_desk.book` (admin, sales: who talk to agencies). The person who asked
  never decides (403 + CHECK).
- **Fee override after the cancel.** The cancel happens at once at the policy fee (seats back on sale, agency told);
  a lower fee is a separate approval that lowers `fee_amount` (guard trigger: once, lower, in the approving update) and
  emits `booking.fee_changed`. An override is always below the policy fee (0 = waive).
- **Supplier cascade**: pending and requested supplier bookings are cancelled on a case cancel (the issue's rule; a
  request is withdrawn and staff tell the supplier), confirmed ones raise "cancel with supplier".
- **Supplier cancel fee** is a planned cost: `v_cost_variance.cost_usd` of a cancelled, unbilled booking = the
  recorded fee at the rate of the cancel day; an approved bill still wins.
- **Partial cancellation is an amendment record, not a new version.** bk-003 locks an approved version and allows one
  approved version per case, so "a new approved version" would need the lock changed for every case. The reduction rows
  carry the removed share, the fee and the refundable part; the confirmation, manifest, vouchers, settlement draft,
  later cancel and profit view read them. Only per-person lines (tour, activity, entrance, meal); rooms, transfers and
  guides are re-quoted. The removed share is proportional to seats (adults + children); infants hold no seat.
- **Partial cancel on the agency API too** (contract 1.7), with the same service as the staff route.
- **Child / infant on the sell side.** The components price every adult and child alike; `child_pct` scales the tour
  line's sell price, `infant_flat_usd` adds a net per infant priced by the rule. Component lines and the agency tariff
  are unchanged (the issue: "GET /tariff and price.from.v1 unchanged for adults"); the CRM gets the child / infant
  price from the quote line's `b2b_price` of its pax split.
- **Per-tour terms are a new table** (`trip_plan_billing_terms`), not a `trip_plan_id` column on `billing_terms`:
  `v_tenant_billing_status` (read by CIS) and the reminder job read `billing_terms` per agency and would take a tour
  row for an agency row. No effective window (billing_terms has none either); a change applies to drafts from then on.
- **Vouchers carry no phone or e-mail.** The root contract field policy refuses contact fields in `booking.b2b.v1`;
  the voucher names the supplier and its confirmation ref, and prints AA's ops desk line from the setting
  `b2b_ops_contact` (`{"label": ...}`, empty until the business owner gives the company line).
- **Manifest step 2 (names) not built**: it needs the DM-6.8 / ADR 0019 amendment the issue names (business owner
  question 9 of the S275 Jira draft). Step 1 is counts only.

## Changed (vs the issue text)

- "trip_cases.source_system stays 'crm' path semantics with a new column entered_via" — done; the staff route is
  `POST /booking/b2b-desk/entries` (not `/b2b-desk/bookings`, which already names a confirmed booking by case ref).
- "new approved version" on a partial cancel — replaced by the reduction record (see Decisions).
- "booking.billing_terms gains nullable trip_plan_id" — a separate table (see Decisions).
- Voucher "supplier booking phone" and "AA 24h contact" — no contact fields (contract field policy); a label setting.
- Manifest step 2 — not built (needs the data-standard amendment).

## Tradeoffs

- Seat-proportional removed share is simple and auditable; with a child share the child's seat is worth less than an
  adult's, so removing a child refunds slightly more than its price. Accepted until the quote line stores per-type
  prices.
- A "full" settlement line billed before a partial cancellation is not credited automatically (one live line per
  hold and role); finance adds an adjustment on the draft. Same for a cancellation fee billed before an approved
  override.
- `lapsed` has no job: the list computes it from the hold; the row is written `lapsed` when someone tries to decide.
