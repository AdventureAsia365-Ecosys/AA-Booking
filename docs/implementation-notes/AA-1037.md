
# AA-1037 R4 slice 1: agency billing core (S269, lane l8)

Root ADR 0019 d8 / d9, b14 / b16 / b17; design: root `docs/audits/2026-11-03-S265-ecosystem-restructure/clarifications.md`
TL-8, TL-9, BO-14..17. This slice: the billing tables, the two draft jobs, the staff admin pages and the billing status
CIS reads. Not in this slice: the B2C drop, the `trip_cases` channel CHECK (a later DRY RUN slice), partner tiers and
`partner_terms` locked on a hold (AA-1030 and a later R4 slice), the provider e-invoice API.

## Decisions

- **Two Booking files.** bk-041 is expand (seven new tables, their indexes and CHECKs, two seed rows into tables it
  creates, grants to `booking`). Everything the expand lint refuses goes to bk-042, contract: the guard / settle
  triggers (no `CREATE TRIGGER IF NOT EXISTS`, and `DROP` / `CREATE OR REPLACE` are not expand), the `REVOKE DELETE`
  (default privileges give `booking` DELETE on every new table), the view (needs `REVOKE INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE`) and the
  `cis_app` grant. The code reads both, so readiness goes to bk-042 in the code PR (MIG-4).
- **Kinds never mix (b17) by a composite FK**, not a trigger: `agency_invoice_line (invoice_id, invoice_kind)`
  references `agency_invoice (id, invoice_kind)`, and the role CHECK lists the roles of each kind.
- **Gap-free numbers** without a sequence object: `invoice_sequence (series, year, last_no)`, taken by
  `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE last_no + 1 RETURNING` inside the issue transaction. The row lock serialises issues of
  one series and a rollback gives the number back. Numbers are given at **issue**, never to drafts, so deleting a draft
  never leaves a hole; a void invoice keeps its number.
- **Money state derived by the database** (like bk-028): an approval that covers the total marks the invoice `paid`
  (`agency_payment_settle`); the invoice guard refuses `paid` without that money and refuses `void` once money is
  approved. Approvals can never exceed the invoice total (the payment guard locks the invoice row first).
- **Maker-checker** is a CHECK (`approved_by` / `rejected_by` <> `recorded_by`) plus the API's 403 `maker_checker`.
  Approve and reject are `agency_payment.approve` = admin + finance (the finance role of bk-038), so two finance users
  can run the whole flow without an admin. `document_ref` is NOT NULL (the evidence: statement line, slip number or an
  ops file id).
- **Settlement lines follow the payment terms** (b16) instead of billing the whole booking at once: per confirmed B2B
  hold, a `deposit` (deposit % of the B2B total) when the hold was confirmed in the period and the departure is more
  than `balance_days_before_departure` away, else `full`; a `balance` once the balance date falls in the period; a
  cancelled booking gets one `cancellation_fee` line = the fee less what was already billed (a credit when negative).
  One live line per (hold, role) (partial unique index), so a re-run never bills twice. Events dated before the period
  that were never billed land on the next run (the candidate query is "unbilled, dated before the period end").
- **SaaS lines** come from the CIS subscription through a new CIS catalog view `catalog.v_tenant_billing_v1` (CIS 381):
  `catalog.v_tenant_account_v1` has no plan, price or bill-to fields and `tenant.entitlements.v1` carries limits, not
  prices. Live subscriptions only (`active`, `past_due`); a plan without a price (Enterprise, contract price) gives a
  zero line flagged `price_missing` for finance to adjust on the draft.
- **Drafts only.** Both jobs write drafts; issuing (number, snapshots, due date) is a person's act. One live invoice
  per (agency, kind, period start, currency): the job adds missing lines to an existing draft and leaves an issued one.
- **Billing status** (`booking.v_tenant_billing_status`): ok / warn / past_due from the oldest overdue issued invoice
  with a balance against the agency's warn / suspend days. With no terms row at all it says `warn` once overdue and
  never `past_due` (no suspension without a configured number).
- **Config is data, edited in the admin**: billing entity, tax rules, default terms and per-agency overrides
  (`billing_config.write` = admin, finance; audited). Maker-checker on these is the "editable Settings" slice of R4.

## Changed

- **A CIS migration was needed** (CIS 381, `catalog.v_tenant_billing_v1`): the issue says "from the existing
  `catalog.v_tenant_account_v1` / `tenant.entitlements` contract"; neither has plans, prices or bill-to fields, so the
  contract `tenant.entitlements.v1` gains a third view part (1.3, root + CIS copy, SHA pin updated).
- **The view carries more than `status` / `oldest_overdue_days`**: also overdue invoices, overdue amount per currency,
  `paid_through` (ADR 0019 d9 names it) and the warn / suspend days, so CIS needs no second read.
- **The settlement job follows the deposit / balance terms** (b16) instead of one line per booking; a voided deposit is
  re-billed as "total less the billed balance" (found by the integration test: the first version lost the deposit).
- **Schema model names** in the router carry an `Agency` / `Billing` prefix: FastAPI renames clashing Pydantic models
  (`InvoiceChanges`, `NewPayment`, `ReasonIn` exist in `routers/billing.py`), which would have changed the published
  OpenAPI of the B2C routes.
- Staff pages: one route `/agency-billing` with four tabs (Invoices, Payments to approve, Agencies, Settings) and
  `/agency-billing/invoices/[ref]`; nav entry under Operations, admin + finance only.

## Tradeoffs

- No maker-checker on terms / tax edits in this slice (audited instead), to keep the slice shippable; the clarifications
  ask for a second approver on policy edits after R4.
- The settlement period is a calendar month (UTC) and invoices are per currency of the B2B quote.
