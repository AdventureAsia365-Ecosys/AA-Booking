# AA-1149 — A sold tour becomes one supplier booking per component, and a can-operate check

Part 1 of the issue (S288 lane): T15 (snapshot at sale and the split), B13 (can-operate check with the admin sale
exception) and the B12 label. Migration bk-065 (migration-only PR first, MIG-4), then the code PR on top. Part 2 (T16
typed service attributes and editable quantity, the rest of B12, see "Should know") stays open on the issue.

## Decisions

- **The snapshot is the quote calculation itself** (`booking.b2b_quote_line_parts`, append-only), written in the
  transaction that stores the quote. The hold confirms one fixed quote, so the parts of the confirmed quote are what
  was sold; nothing is copied at confirm time (`app/b2b/holds.py` is at its file-size ratchet). Staff desk bookings go
  through the same quote, so they are covered too.
- **Component bookings point at their part** (`supplier_bookings.quote_part_id`) with a new partial unique index.
  **The old `supplier_bookings_item_live_uq` stays untouched** although the issue text says "the unique index moves to
  per component": changing its predicate would break the running code's `INSERT … ON CONFLICT (version_item_id) WHERE …`
  inference during the MIG-4 window and fail the migration-only PR's integration tests. The new index gives the same
  guarantee for parts, and bk-065 stays purely additive.
- **Which parts count** (supply view and Generate): parts of hold lines that are `confirmed` (an on-request line only
  after the desk accepted it, so a later decline never leaves live component bookings behind) and whose quote line has
  no departure (a departure's costs are booked once per departure, `supplier_bookings.departure_id`). Parts of
  departure lines are still stored.
- **A part can be closed** with a waiver (`booking.trip_case_part_waivers`, reason 20..500 characters, audit row),
  otherwise a part nobody can book would block `pre_departure` for good.
- **One booking per component** (no merging of consecutive hotel nights): the Done-when counts 10 bookings for a 5-day
  tour. Revisit with T16.
- **A part whose rates are in more than one currency** stores a NULL net pair (the booking is generated without a net
  and staff enter it) instead of refusing the sale, splitting the part or converting to USD: no guessed net, no lost
  sale.
- **The room mix is kept on the part** (`rooms`: occupancy names and counts as asked, or the default doubles + single),
  so the booking says "Double ×2 + Single ×1", not only "3 rooms".
- **Sale exception** (`booking.trip_plan_operable_overrides`) covers exact findings of N1 (night without a hotel) and N3
  (supplier not active) only. A missing rate (N2) or an inactive room type, hotel or service is refused by the quote
  whatever an exception says (`price_lines`), so the exception cannot cover them; the API and the drawer say so.
- **The capability is approve class**: `operable_exception.approve` (admin only). The name matches
  `custom_roles.APPROVE_SUFFIX` and the bk-062 CHECK, so a custom role can never be given it, with no DB change.
- **Which exception covered a sale** is recorded on the quote line (`b2b_quote_lines.operable_override_id`), only when
  the exception covered a finding of that line. The ops task is a trip check added by a trigger when the hold becomes
  confirmed (fires once per hold), not at Generate: the issue says trips sold under it get a task.
- **Policy values** `operable_exception_default_days` (14) and `operable_exception_max_days` (30) live in the existing
  section "operations", approver group admin, in `policy_params_ops.py` / `policy_guides_ops.py` (no new modules); a
  cross-check keeps default ≤ max. The DB keeps a 90-day outer backstop (`technical-constant`).

## Tradeoffs

- Quote parts vs a case-level copy at confirm: parts need no change to `holds.py` and are exactly what was priced;
  the price is a second table keyed by the quote.
- Trigger vs code for the exception task: the trigger also covers the staff desk confirm and needs no line moves in
  `holds.py`; its text is English SQL (the trip check title / note).
- The can-operate check runs at quote time only. Hold place and confirm do not re-check it (a quote is valid
  `quote_validity_days`); recorded in CONTEXT.md.

  plan has a hotel only on day 1 (or none) is refused with N1 until its nights get hotels, "No hotel needed" marks or a
  sale exception. Check `/pricing/readiness?operable=blocked` and the E2E synthetic-supply tours before the CRM E2E runs.

  AA-1157 "POST under view-as is 403" case for `/sell-exception` once that lands (not testable on this branch).

  `tsc`, ESLint, the frontend unit tests and the code-health ratchet are green. CI runs the build.

- bk-065 is numbered after AA-1158's bk-064 (this branch sits on it). Seed ids `…0000b65000…` do not collide with
  bk-064's `…0000b64000…`.
