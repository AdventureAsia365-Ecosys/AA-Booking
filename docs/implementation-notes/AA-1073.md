# AA-1073 — Booking departures: minimum to operate, sales cutoff, recurring generator, agency allotments

Lane l19 of session S277 (Claude Code). Source of the scope: Linear AA-1073 and the S275 study (root
`docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md`, items bk-01, bk-02, bk-03). Our own code; nothing of
the OPL-1 app is copied. This lane builds the first three items of the issue; the departure board (bk-04), the ops
sheet (bk-16), departure fees (bk-09) and price adjustments (bk-17) are later slices of the same issue.

## Decisions

- **One migration, bk-046, contract.** It ALTERs `booking.departures` (four columns + CHECKs), replaces the
  `departures_outbox` trigger and the outbox `event_type` CHECK, next to one new table `booking.departure_allotments`.
  So it is MIG-4: a migration-only PR, then the code PR raises readiness to bk-046. A split into a contract file and an
  expand file (the table) was weighed and dropped: the table's outbox trigger needs the new event type, so both would
  have to apply together anyway.
- **`min_to_operate = 0` means "no minimum"** (manual, as before bk-046): the watch never auto-guarantees nor flags such
  a departure. Without this rule the first watch run would guarantee every scheduled departure (sold >= 0) and tell
  every agency on them. Proposed defaults kept from the issue: cutoff 0 days, decision 21 days before start.
- **Sales cutoff**: sales close on `start_date - sales_cutoff_days` (that day included). Cutoff 0 = the old rule (a
  departure starting today or earlier is sold out). The B2B answer says why: `sales closed on <date>`; the contract
  1.6 `Departure.last_sale_date` is the day before the close.
- **At risk** is a fact stamped once (`departures.at_risk_at`) by the daily watch on the decision date
  (`start_date - decision_days_before`) while the departure is scheduled with fewer sold seats than its minimum. The
  stamp fires the outbox event `departure.at_risk` (trigger, like `departure.guaranteed`), one staff notification
  (shared queue, once per departure) and an audit row. The desk lists a departure as at risk while it is scheduled,
  stamped and still short of its minimum; staff decide there: "Run anyway" (guaranteed) or "Cancel departure". The
  third choice of the issue, "charge shortfall", needs the departure fees of bk-09 (later slice).
- **Auto-guarantee**: the daily watch moves a scheduled departure with `min_to_operate >= 1` and sold >= minimum to
  `guaranteed` (the existing trigger emits `departure.guaranteed`). Sold = confirmed holds whose line is not declined
  / cancelled, never held seats. Started departures are left alone.
- **Allotments** (`booking.departure_allotments`): append-only rows, maker-checker (a second staff member approves;
  CHECK `approved_by <> created_by`). A change is a new pending row naming the one it `replaces`; approving it ends
  the old row (`end_reason = replaced`) in the same transaction, so at most one allotment per agency and departure is
  in force (partial unique index) and one pending. Ending: `rejected` (pending), `withdrawn` (in force),
  `departure_cancelled` (cancelling a departure ends its live allotments). Released = `released_at` stamped by the job
  `bk_allotment_release` on `start_date - release_days_before` (UTC day); the row stays (history) but reserves nothing.
- **Seat accounting with allotments** (counted, never stored, like held / sold): for each agency with an allotment in
  force and not released, `unused = max(seats - its held + sold on the departure, 0)`. General free seats =
  `capacity - held - sold - sum(unused)`. An agency sees (quotes, holds, `GET /departures`) the general free seats
  plus its own unused allotment, i.e. `capacity - held - sold - unused of the OTHER agencies`. Its holds draw from its
  allotment first by construction (its own seats are in its own count).
- **Approval check under the departure lock**: approving locks the departure row (the same lock as holds), counts,
  and refuses (409 `allotment_exceeds_free`) when the general free seats would go below zero. A capacity edit is
  refused below held + sold + unused allotments (409 `capacity_below_taken`).
- **Generator**: a pure function builds the dates (weekly on ISO weekdays 1..7, or monthly on a day 1..31: a month
  without that day is skipped, never moved), at most 400 days and 400 dates, all after today (UTC). Insert with
  `ON CONFLICT (trip_plan_id, start_date) DO NOTHING`; the answer lists created and skipped dates; one audit row per
  batch. `dry_run` reads which dates exist and writes nothing. Permission `trip_plan.write` (admin, contracting), the
  same as creating one departure (the issue said catalog.admin / pricing.write; keeping the departure permission
  avoids a second rule for the same rows).
- **Jobs**: `bk_departure_watch` and `bk_allotment_release`, queued once per UTC day by `DepartureDailySchedule`
  (first pass at or after 01:00 UTC, key per kind and day), and by staff `POST /booking/b2b-desk/departure-checks/runs`
  (b2b_desk.write; key per kind and UTC minute; `dry_run` for the watch). Not deploy-gate exempt (seconds).
- **Contract booking.b2b.v1 1.6** (additive, root docs/contracts): `Departure` gains `min_to_operate`,
  `seats_to_guarantee`, `last_sale_date` and `allotment` (the caller's own, else null); `available` is what the caller
  can hold (own unused allotment included); events `departure.at_risk` and `allotment.released`. The AA-Partner CRM
  is a tolerant reader (an unknown event type is recorded as ignored), so the feed change breaks nothing there.

## Changed (against the issue text)

- Permission of the generator: `trip_plan.write` instead of catalog.admin / pricing.write (see Decisions).
- Allotment maker and checker both need `b2b_desk.write` (admin, sales, contracting), two different people. No new
  permission row.
- No settings rows: the defaults are column defaults (0 / 0 / 21).

## Tradeoffs

- The watch is daily, so a departure that reaches its minimum is guaranteed by the next run, not at the confirm. A
  hook in the confirm path would be immediate but adds a status move (and an agency event) inside the hold
  transaction; the issue asked for a daily job and staff can run it at once from the desk.
- Release on the UTC day: the ops time zone (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh) is 7 h ahead, so a release can land up to 7 h "late"
  in local time. Same convention as the hold expiry and the case lifecycle.
