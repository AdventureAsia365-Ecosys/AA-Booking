# AA-1116 — option B on the monthly software draft (slice S7, discount lines S283)

The CIS part (migrations 435-439, contract tenant.entitlements.v1 1.9) is live on Dev. This slice makes
`bk_saas_invoice_draft` read the terms CIS 438 appends to `catalog.v_tenant_billing_v1`. No migration.

## Decisions

- **Same rules as the CIS quote** (`services/tenant_account/quote.py` in AA-CIS-App): currency = the agency's
  `billing_currency`; USD lines from the USD list, VND lines from the VND list; per-seat plans bill
  max(seats, seats_min, 1); other plans bill the plan fee plus `extra_seats` at the extra-seat list price; an agreed
  price in the invoice currency replaces the list UNIT price while `agreed_until` is empty or not before the period
  start. The quote reads `agreed_until` against today; the draft reads it against the period start (the schedule drafts
  on the period's first day, and a staff re-run of an older month must price that month).
- **Fallback before CIS 438**: the 438 columns are feature-detected on their own (`cis.TERMS_VIEW`); without them every
  row carries them as `None` and the draft keeps the Booking `saas_currency` and the USD list (377 behaviour).
- **Free pilot drafted at 0** (`bill_free`): every line kept with unit price 0 and the list amount in its text
  ("pilot, free (list 3,790,000 VND)"), counted `pilot_free`. Drafting at 0 instead of skipping keeps one draft per
  agency and month (a re-run after go-live replaces the zero lines with the price) and shows finance the pilot.
- **Annual subscriptions** (`billing_interval = 'year'`): a 0 line "annual: invoice by hand", counted `annual_manual`;
  no annual drafting (S282 decision 7).
- **Discounts flagged, not computed** (decision 7; review fix; REPLACED in S283, see "S283: discount lines" below): the founding offer dates and the combo set are not in
  the CIS view, so Booking cannot price a discount. A draft read with the CIS 438 terms whose lines have a positive
  amount ends with a 0 line "Discount: check the Tenant 360 quote for a founding offer or combo discount and add it as
  an adjustment" (role `subscription`, the first priced line's product / plan, replaced on a re-run like the other job
  lines), counted `discount_check` (one per agency). Not added to a draft with nothing to discount (free pilot, annual
  only, price missing only) nor before CIS 438 (no option B discount then). Reason: the studio Billing tab shows the
  CIS quote total (discount included), so a silent list-price draft would disagree with what the agency sees.
  Follow-up: CIS appends the quote's discount (kind, pct, founding window) to `catalog.v_tenant_billing_v1`
  (contract 1.10); then the draft writes the discount line itself and the check line goes.
- **Agreed price** lines say "agreed price until <date>", counted `agreed` (a discounted term finance can check).
- **Currency change**: when CIS moves an agency to another currency, the job lines of that month's draft in the old
  currency are removed (counted `currency_moved`) so one month never has two live software fees. When the month's
  software invoice is already issued (or paid) in the old currency (review fix), the run drafts nothing in the new
  currency: `period_issued` + `currency_moved_issued`. Finance voids the issued invoice and the next run drafts the
  new currency; the next month is drafted in the new currency as usual.
- **Trialing** subscriptions stay out of the draft (only `active`, `past_due`, as before): not billed during the trial.

## Changed

- `app/agency_billing/cis.py`: `TERMS_COLUMNS`, `TERMS_VIEW`; `subscriptions()` selects them when present.
- `app/agency_billing/drafts.py`: `saas_lines(subs, period_start, currency)` returns `(lines, flags)` (was
  `(lines, missing)`); `run_saas` reads the currency from CIS, refuses only currencies CIS has no list for
  (`SAAS_CURRENCIES` USD / VND), counts `agreed`, `annual_manual`, `pilot_free`, `currency_moved`.
- Review fixes: `ISSUED_OTHER_CURRENCY_SQL` guard before any draft write; `DISCOUNT_CHECK_TEXT` line and the
  `discount_check` / `currency_moved_issued` counts (unit + pg tests, including "issued USD, then VND").
- Staff admin: Agency billing KPI tiles list every currency (were USD only); the terms currency field says it is set in
  CIS Tenant 360.

## Tradeoffs

- `SAAS_CURRENCIES` is a technical constant (the currencies `shared.plan` has price columns for), not a policy value.
- A trial ending mid-month is billed from the next month's draft (start-of-period billing, as before).

## Should know

- No migration; deploy when `booking.job` is idle (DM-11.4). Amounts of VND agencies change from "not drafted" to a
  VND draft on the next daily run.
- Live check: a synthetic VND agency (Tenant 360 billing currency VND, a plan with a VND price) → run the SaaS draft for
  it (`POST /booking/agency-billing/runs`) → the draft is in VND with the VND list price.

## S283: discount lines (contract 1.10, CIS 443-444)

CIS 444 appends `discount_pct` numeric(5,2) and `discount_kind` (founding | combo) to `catalog.v_tenant_billing_v1`:
the share of THIS row's amount the CIS quote takes off. The zero "check the Tenant 360 quote" line is gone.

### Decisions

- **One negative `adjustment` line per discounted subscription**, right after its subscription / extra-seat lines:
  `-(discount_pct x what that subscription bills)`, text "Combo discount 10%: <plan> (<product>), <month>" (or
  "Founding offer discount"). The quote's line amount includes the extra seats, so the share covers both lines.
- **Role `adjustment`, no migration**: bk-041/048 allow a negative unit price only on `adjustment` and
  `cancellation_fee`; `subscription` / `extra_seats` need `unit_price >= 0`. A new `discount` role would be a contract
  migration (CHECK swap) for no gain. The line carries the `product` and no `plan_key` (the source check keeps plan
  keys for subscription / extra-seat lines).
- **The job's own discount line = `adjustment` with a product.** Finance's adjustments (`invoices.add_line`) never
  set one, so `DELETE_JOB_LINES_SQL` removes the job's discount lines with its subscription lines on a re-run and on a
  currency move, and keeps finance's credits. Chosen over the `created_by` prefix (`job:`) because it does not depend
  on how a run is started.
- **Rounding like the quote**: the quote sums the exact shares and rounds once (USD cents, VND dong, `PLACES`). The
  draft keeps the exact running total and gives each line the rounded running total less what the earlier lines took,
  so the discount lines add up to the quote's rounded discount (3 x 4.16625 = 12.50, not 3 x 4.17 = 12.51).
- **Nothing billed, nothing discounted**: a free pilot (`bill_free`), an annual line and a missing price bill 0, so no
  discount line (contract 1.10: a pilot with bill_free stays unbilled). An agreed price is discounted like the list
  price (the quote does the same).
- **Feature-detected on its own** (`cis.DISCOUNT_VIEW`, only checked when the 438 terms are readable): before 444 the
  rows carry the two columns as `None` and the draft has no discount line.
- Run count `discount_check` renamed `discounted` (subscriptions with a discount line); no UI read the old key.

### Changed

- `app/agency_billing/cis.py`: `DISCOUNT_COLUMNS`, `DISCOUNT_VIEW`, `OPTIONAL_COLUMNS`; `subscriptions()` selects the
  444 columns when present.
- `app/agency_billing/drafts.py`: `DISCOUNT_CHECK_TEXT` removed; `PLACES`, `DISCOUNT_LABEL`, `_discount_line`;
  `saas_lines` writes the discount lines; `DELETE_JOB_LINES_SQL` also removes the job's discount adjustments.
- Tests: unit (409.50 combo case, founding on a plan fee with extra seats and an agreed price, running-total rounding,
  VND, nothing billed, before 444, re-run SQL, issued USD month then VND next month) and PostgreSQL (the view stood in
  with the exact 444 columns; 409.50; a re-run keeps a finance credit; CIS dropping the discount removes the lines;
  the currency moves keep no old-currency discount line; December drafted in VND with its VND discount).

### Tradeoffs

- The share is CIS's (stored per subscription by the CIS code, refreshed hourly and on each Tenant 360 billing
  write). Booking does not re-derive founding / combo eligibility; a combo counted with a product that only trials
  in CIS still discounts the billed lines, as the quote does.
- A discount line finance removes by hand comes back on the next daily run, like any job line.

### Should know

- No migration, no new job kind, no new threshold (the percentages are CIS Platform settings). Deploy when
  `booking.job` is idle (DM-11.4).
- Until the CIS AA-1116 code that fills `shared.tenant_subscription.quote_discount_pct` runs its first tick, every
  row says 0 and the draft has no discount line (and no check line any more).
- Live check: an agency whose Tenant 360 quote shows a discount -> `POST /booking/agency-billing/runs` (saas, the
  current month, that tenant) -> the draft total equals the quote's monthly total and has one "... discount N%: ..."
  line per discounted subscription.


## S284: the seat-overage line on a per-seat plan (lane l12)

The S282 progress report (G13) found no line for the seats above what a per-seat plan includes (CRM Core / Pro: price
per seat, 3 included and minimum): the draft billed every seat on one `subscription` line, and the plan's extra-seat
price was ignored on such a plan. The CIS quote gets the same split in the same wave (AA-CIS-App
`services/tenant_account/quote.py` `seat_split`), so the draft and the agency's Billing tab still agree.

### Decisions

- **Split** (`drafts.seat_split`, same table test as the CIS quote): the billed seats stay max(seats, `seats_min`, 1);
  the `subscription` line bills the seats the plan includes, max(`seats_included`, `seats_min`, 1) (capped at the
  billed seats), at the seat price; an `extra_seats` line bills the rest. No `seats_included` on the plan = one line as
  before.
- **Extra-seat price**: the plan's extra-seat list price in the invoice currency (`price_extra_seat_usd` /
  `price_extra_seat_vnd`, CIS Plans page), else the seat price, so a plan without one bills the same total as before
  (option B left them empty on the CRM plans). An agreed price in force covers the extra seats (the locked seat price,
  CIS 435 comment). No literal, no setting: both prices are plan rows read from `catalog.v_tenant_billing_v1`.
- **No migration**: `extra_seats` is an existing line role (bk-041) with a plan key; the discount line already takes
  its share of every line of its subscription; a free pilot keeps both lines at zero with the list amounts in the text.

### Changed

- `backend/app/agency_billing/drafts.py`: `seat_split`, `_priced_lines(..., agreed=)`.
- `backend/tests/unit/test_aa1116_saas_draft.py`: the overage at the seat price and at the extra-seat price, VND, no
  extra seats at or under the included ones, the agreed price, the discount over both lines, the free pilot, the split
  table.

### Should know

- Seats are the subscription's seats on CIS Tenant 360 (what the agency pays for; the CRM's member limit). CRM members
  in use above them are not billed: no contract carries the CRM's seat use to Booking (follow-up in the CIS notes
  `docs/implementation-notes/AA-1116-l12-seats.md`).
- Live check: a synthetic agency with CRM seats 5 (plan includes 3) -> `POST /booking/agency-billing/runs` (saas, the
  current month, that tenant) -> the draft has "..., 3 seats" (3 x seat price) and "Extra seats over 3, ..." (2 x the
  extra-seat or seat price), and its total equals the Tenant 360 quote.
