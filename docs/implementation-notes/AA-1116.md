# AA-1116 — option B on the monthly software draft (slice S7)

The CIS part (migrations 435-439, contract tenant.entitlements.v1 1.9) is live on Dev. This slice makes
`bk_saas_invoice_draft` read the terms CIS 438 appends to `catalog.v_tenant_billing_v1`. No migration.

## Decisions

- **Same rules as the CIS quote** (`services/tenant_account/quote.py` in AA-CIS-App): currency = the agency's
  `billing_currency`; USD lines from the USD list, VND lines from the VND list; per-seat plans bill
  max(seats, seats_min, 1); other plans bill the plan fee plus `extra_seats` at the extra-seat list price; an agreed
  price in the invoice currency replaces the list UNIT price while `agreed_until` is empty or not before the period
  start. The quote reads `agreed_until` against today; the draft reads it against the period start (the schedule drafts
  on the period's first day, and a staff re-run of an older month must price that month).
- **Fallback before CIS 438**: the 438 columns are feature-detected on their own (`cis.TERMS_VIEW`); without them every
  row carries them as `None` and the draft keeps the Booking `saas_currency` and the USD list (377 behaviour).
- **Free pilot drafted at 0** (`bill_free`): every line kept with unit price 0 and the list amount in its text
  ("pilot, free (list 3,790,000 VND)"), counted `pilot_free`. Drafting at 0 instead of skipping keeps one draft per
  agency and month (a re-run after go-live replaces the zero lines with the price) and shows finance the pilot.
- **Annual subscriptions** (`billing_interval = 'year'`): a 0 line "annual: invoice by hand", counted `annual_manual`;
  no annual drafting (S282 decision 7).
- **No discount lines** (decision 7): the founding offer dates and the combo set are not in the CIS view, so Booking
  cannot see a discount. Finance applies it by hand from the Tenant 360 quote. Follow-up: CIS appends the quote's
  discount (kind, pct) to the view (contract 1.10) if the drafts should carry it.
- **Agreed price** lines say "agreed price until <date>", counted `agreed` (a discounted term finance can check).
- **Currency change**: when CIS moves an agency to another currency, the job lines of that month's draft in the old
  currency are removed (counted `currency_moved`) so one month never has two live software fees; an already issued
  invoice in the old currency stays for finance to void.
- **Trialing** subscriptions stay out of the draft (only `active`, `past_due`, as before): not billed during the trial.

## Changed

- `app/agency_billing/cis.py`: `TERMS_COLUMNS`, `TERMS_VIEW`; `subscriptions()` selects them when present.
- `app/agency_billing/drafts.py`: `saas_lines(subs, period_start, currency)` returns `(lines, flags)` (was
  `(lines, missing)`); `run_saas` reads the currency from CIS, refuses only currencies CIS has no list for
  (`SAAS_CURRENCIES` USD / VND), counts `agreed`, `annual_manual`, `pilot_free`, `currency_moved`.
- Staff admin: Agency billing KPI tiles list every currency (were USD only); the terms currency field says it is set in
  CIS Tenant 360.

## Tradeoffs

- `SAAS_CURRENCIES` is a technical constant (the currencies `shared.plan` has price columns for), not a policy value.
- A trial ending mid-month is billed from the next month's draft (start-of-period billing, as before).
